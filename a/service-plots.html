Hunting Feeding Plots

Installed plots with a soil number, a seed plan, and a first mow — sized to the farm, not a social post.

We do not drop a mix on sod and call it a plot. Typical install: soil test, lime/fert as indicated, kill or till, drill or broadcast, pack, and a 30-day check. Brassica, cereal, clover, and mixed hides are all in play — you pick the hunt calendar, we pick the rate.

  • ½-acre kill plots up to multi-acre destination fields
  • Screening cover on the approach if the field is naked
  • Sample from $1,150 / acre installed (seed mix may be extra)
  • Re-seed and mow can be scheduled as a second-year add
