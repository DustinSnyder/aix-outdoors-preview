Overgrown land is not a mood. It is lost access, poor visibility, and acreage you cannot walk, plant, or maintain. We open it back up — hunting ground, plots, and the driveway included, not mulching alone.

Sound familiar?

What landowners ask

Full FAQ →

What drives the cost?

Acreage, density, terrain, access, stone index, and the finish you want (mulch in place, plot, trail, driveway). This sample publishes ranges so you can sanity-check — nothing here is a bid.

How long does a job take?

A small fence-line or stand-lane day can finish in a weather window. Multi-acre clearing, plots, or a long gravel lift take more crew days. The quote names a start week, not a vibe.

Do you haul brush away?

Default on clearing is mulching in place as a natural mulch layer — no burn pile unless you ask and permit it. Haul-off, stone delivery, and load-and-go are the dump-truck line.

Can you work steep or wet ground?

Tracked machines, and remote-capable gear where the bank or the wet draw needs it. If we cannot reach it safely, we say so in the quote instead of tearing a rut you will hate in April.
