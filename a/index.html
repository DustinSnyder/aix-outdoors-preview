Who we are

Southeast Iowa land management for rural property owners

Practical, results-driven outdoor services — invasive clearing, gravel driveway maintenance, and timber stand improvement.

AIX Outdoors LLC is a southeast Iowa land management company specializing in practical, results-driven outdoor services for rural property owners. We focus on clearing invasive plants that choke out native habitat, maintaining gravel driveways so they stay functional year-round, and performing timber stand improvement that strengthens the health and long-term value of your woods.

Based in the Burlington / New London area and serving southeast Iowa, we combine the right equipment with local knowledge of Iowa soils, vegetation, and seasons. Landowners hire us for clean, professional results that leave the land far better than we found it.

Invasive plant clearing

We remove aggressive invasive species common across Iowa — multiflora rose, bush honeysuckle, autumn olive, and other brush that spreads quickly and reduces forage and cover for wildlife. Efficient clearing while protecting desirable trees and understory so native habitat recovers stronger.

Gravel driveway maintenance

We regrade, reshape, and re-surface driveways to improve drainage, reduce washouts, and keep access reliable through wet seasons and freeze-thaw cycles.

Timber stand improvement

Selective thinning, removing competing or low-value stems, and opening the canopy to favor higher-quality hardwoods and better wildlife habitat — tailored to your woods and your goals.

Why landowners call

Problems landowners hire us for

If your property is stuck in one of these, that is the job — we bring the machines and finish the work on your land.

Invasive brush choking habitat

Multiflora rose, bush honeysuckle, autumn olive, and other invasives crowding out native habitat along fence lines and timber edges you cannot walk or mow.

Washed-out gravel driveway

Potholes, no crown, poor drainage — washouts after rain and freeze-thaw that leave your lane unreliable through wet seasons.

Woods that need TSI

Crowded canopy, competing low-value stems, and little light for higher-quality hardwoods or wildlife cover.

No usable trails

No quiet way in, mud holes, ruts, or a cut that erodes every spring.

Plots that failed

Wrong spot, wrong wind, poor soil, or a seed-bag layout that never hunted.

Debris to haul

Nowhere to dump on site, stone that has to come from a pit, spoil with no home.
