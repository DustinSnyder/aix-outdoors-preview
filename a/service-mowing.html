Sound familiar?

A field left alone doesn’t stay a field.

  • CRP-style ground going from grass to brush to saplings in a few seasons
  • Roadsides and field edges nobody has time to keep up
  • Seasonal setbacks and cover requirements that need a specific cutting window, not a random Saturday
  • Growth too rough, too thick, or too many acres for a lawn tractor

More than “just mow it”

Mowing and mulching are different tools for different growth — using the wrong one wastes a trip.

A rotary or batwing mower knocks back grass, forbs, and small stems in one pass and is priced by the acre. Once brush and saplings get past a certain stem diameter, mowing just bounces off them instead of cutting them, and a forestry mulching head is the right tool instead. We also set the height left on purpose — a wildlife-cover cut and a clean hay-field look are different requests with different blade settings.

When mowing is the wrong tool

Brush and saplings beyond mowing size need to move to the clearing line, not get mowed twice and called done. We’ll say that on the walk-through so you’re not paying for a pass that won’t hold.
