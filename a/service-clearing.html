Forestry Mulching & Brush Clearing

Forestry mulching and brush clearing for rural acreage — invasive thickets, fence rows, and timber edges ground in place so you can mow, plant, or walk again. No burn pile left for you to deal with.

We specialize in forestry mulching and brush clearing on private land across rural southeast Iowa, northeast Missouri, and west-central / eastern Illinois approaches to our Burlington base. Default finish is mulching in place — a natural mulch layer, not a burn pile. Fence rows that must remain wildlife cover are flagged, not flattened. Tracked machines (remote-capable where the bank or wet draw demands it). Stumps can stay low for a later food plot or come out if a driveway is the real goal.

More than a brush cut

Forestry mulching leaves a finish. A brush-hog pass just knocks stems over.

Forestry mulching grinds invasive brush and woody thickets into a mat that breaks down on site — no burn pile, no windrow. That matters on honeysuckle, autumn olive, buckthorn, and multiflora rose, which rebound fast after a shallow cut. A cheap flail or brush-hog pass knocks stems sideways without finishing the job. We clear selectively: fence-line cover you want to keep gets flagged before the machine gets there.

When clearing is the wrong tool

Mature merchantable timber is not a mulching job — that’s a timber sale or a logger’s call, and we’ll say so instead of running a mulching head through trees worth more standing than ground up. Solid rock benches or heavy stump fields sometimes need an excavator day, not a mulcher pass, and we’ll scope it that way.
