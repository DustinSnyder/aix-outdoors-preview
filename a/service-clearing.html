Ground Clearing

Thickets, hedge, and fence-line junk converted into a surface you can mow, plant, or walk — mulched in place, not left as a burn pile.

We mulch or cut-and-stack according to what the next use needs. Fence rows that must remain wildlife cover are flagged, not flattened. Default is mulching in place as a natural mulch layer. Tracked machines (remote-capable where the bank or wet draw demands it). Stumps can stay low for a later plot or come out if a driveway is the real goal.

  • Brush, locust, multiflora, and volunteer hedge
  • Selective timber edge, not a clearcut unless you ask for one
  • Mulch on-site — haul or burn only if you ask and permit it
  • Finish grade rough-ready for seed or gravel
