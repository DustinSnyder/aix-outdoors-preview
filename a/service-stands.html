Sound familiar?

A stand you can’t get to quietly is a stand you won’t hunt.

  • Noisy approach that announces you before you’re anywhere near the tree
  • No shooting lanes, so the first good buck of the year walks behind cover
  • Stand hung on the wrong wind for the trail that leads to it
  • Overgrown pad or steps that make a quiet climb impossible
  • Gear and cameras scattered with no real plan for wind or the exit route

More than a lane cut

Stand work is about the whole sit, not just clearing a shooting window.

We look at the approach, the prevailing wind, and the exit before we cut anything — then open only the lanes that matter for that stand’s realistic shots. A pad or step gets rebuilt so the climb in is quiet, not just safer. Hardware, cameras, and ladders stay yours; we don’t sell stands in this line, we make the ones you own actually hunt.

When the fix isn’t a lane cut

If the tree, the wind, and the trail genuinely don’t line up, moving the stand is the honest answer. We’ll say that instead of cutting lanes that dress up a location that was never going to hunt well.
