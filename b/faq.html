FAQ

Short answers so the quote form is not doing all the work.

Do you work Cedar Rapids?

No. Cedar Rapids sits outside the 100-mile working radius of Burlington 52601. Iowa City, the Quad Cities, Hannibal, and Quincy are treated as edge jobs and depend on the day’s routing.

Is the pricing on this site a bid?

No. Every figure is a sample range for this design. A bid would be a dated document with acres, linear feet, stone index, and a finish condition.

Will there be an owner photo or bio?

Not in this brand system. AIX Outdoors is presented as a land crew and a radius, not a personality site.

Do you sell hunting stands or seed retail?

No. Stand management is lane, approach, and pad work on equipment you already own. Plot seed is specified in the install, not sold from a shop counter in this sample.

Can you combine services in one mobilization?

Yes — that is the usual quote. Clearing into a plot, or trails plus stand lanes, saves a second farm visit.

What about permits and burns?

Burn windows, county brush rules, and anything touching a public right-of-way stay with the landowner in this sample scope.

How do I know if I am inside 100 miles?

Use the service-area map. If you are on the list, ask. If you are Cedar Rapids, we will decline.
