Ground Clearing

Let light back onto the edge. Clearing is the first habitat decision, not just a cleanup.

A wall of honeysuckle is not cover — it is a dead zone. We open corridors and edges so bedding, browse, and travel make sense again. Residual brush can be left as hinge-cuts where it still hunts.

  • Daylight the interior edge without exposing the bedding
  • Keep mast trees; take the junk that stole their crowns
  • Mulch in place to hold soil on slopes
  • Stage the next use: plot, trail, or simply walkable timber
